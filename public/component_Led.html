Led

Led: Lights emmiting diode, a diode which emmits light when connected to a power source. whose anode pin is always longer that is its cathode pin. It is used in various places LED tv, household Lights and Christmas light due to its energy efficiency

Symbol of an Led

Types Of Leds

Laser Diode

Laser Diode: A Light emitting diode which emits laser light. used in laser cutting eye surgery, CD/DVD drivers and Barcode /QR code scanners.

Infrared Light Diode

IR LED: Infrared Light Emitting diode emits infrared light used in infrared remotes.

RGB Led

Red Green Blue Light Emmitting diode , allows the user to choose their desired colour to emmit by power one of the 3 cathode pins . .RGB LEDs are the most favorite and most popular LEDs among hobbyists and designers.
